# ThunderStudy - Open Educational Study Material & PDF Library
> ThunderStudy provides free high-quality study materials, official NCERT textbooks, topper handwritten notes, previous years' question papers (PYQs), and comprehensive formula handbooks for school education and national competitive examinations in India.

## Core Examination & Subject Portals
- [Home & Catalog](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/): Central repository and exam directory.
- [English Portal](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/english.html): 170+ English grammar, error spotting, vocabulary, reading comprehension passages, and topper answer sheets.
- [Science Chapterwise Notes](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/science/chapterwise-notes.html): 110+ comprehensive physics, chemistry, biology, and earth science notes.
- [Banking Examination Hub](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/banking/): IBPS PO, IBPS RRB, SBI PO, RBI Assistant, and LIC preparation materials.
- [NCERT Books & Solutions](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/ncert/): Official NEP textbooks, exemplar problems, and solutions for Classes 6 to 12.
- [NDA / Defense Exam Hub](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/nda/): Mathematics, GAT English, General Science, and Defense PYQs.
- [SSC & Staff Selection](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/ssc/): SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, and Stenographer notes and PYQs.
- [CLAT & Law Entrance](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/clat/): Legal reasoning, English comprehension, and constitutional law.
- [CAT & MBA Entrance](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/cat/): Quantitative aptitude, VARC reading passages, and DILR modules.
- [UPSC Civil Services](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/upsc/): General studies foundation notes, topper strategies, and NCERT alignments.
- [TET & Teaching Exams](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/tet/): Child Development and Pedagogy (CDP) notes and state TET materials.
- [State Exams (MP & State Services)](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/state/): MP GK, state board guides, and regional exam sets.
- [CA Foundation & Intermediate](https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/ca/): Accounting standards, corporate law, and taxation notes.
